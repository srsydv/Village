Full description
Safar is a private AI travel concierge. Sign in with Google, then ask about destinations, budgets, hotels, packing, and visas, or generate a day-by-day plan before you fly.

What you get
• Ask — short answers on visas, money, packing, and stays
• Plan — an itinerary first, then optional hotel, food, and sight picks
• Trips — save, share, and duplicate plans on any phone you sign in with

Prices are estimates. Hotel and restaurant links open Booking, Airbnb, Zomato, or Maps. Safar does not take payment and does not file visas. You apply on the official site.

Data safety notes
• Google Sign-In is required.
• Chat text and plan details are sent to Google Gemini.
• Account, chats, and trips are stored so they reload on another device.
• Users can delete their account in Profile.
• Not sold. Not used for ads.
