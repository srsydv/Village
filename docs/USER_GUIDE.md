# समूह User Guide

**समूह (Samooh)** is a digital notebook for a village Self-Help Group (SHG). Use it to record weekly savings, loans, and repayments.

It does **not** move money, open bank accounts, or replace cash. Cash still changes hands in the meeting. The app only keeps a clear record.

---

## Before you start

- Use a phone or computer with a browser (Chrome on Android works best).
- Unmute the phone if you want to hear Hindi confirmations.
- The first person (usually the group secretary or facilitator) creates the group and shares the **group code** and **PIN** with trusted members only.

---

## 1. Create a new group

1. Open the app.
2. Tap **नया समूह** (New group).
3. Type the group name (example: फूलमती बचत समूह).
4. Optionally type the village name.
5. Choose a **4-digit PIN**. Remember it. Anyone with the code + PIN can open the ledger.
6. Tap **समूह बनाएं**.

On the home screen you will see:

- The group name
- The **group code** (6 letters/numbers), for example `URDQ8B`

Write the code and PIN in the paper register as well, in case the phone is lost.

## 2. Join an existing group

Use this on a second phone, or if you already have a group.

1. Open the app.
2. Tap **जुड़ें** (Join).
3. Enter the **group code**.
4. Enter the **4-digit PIN**.
5. Tap **अंदर जाएं**.

You will see the same members and the same ledger after sync.

## 3. Add members

1. Tap **समूह** at the bottom, or **सदस्य** on the home screen.
2. Tap **+ जोड़ें**.
3. Type the member’s name.
4. Tap a picture (sun, flower, lotus, and so on) so you can find them quickly without reading.
5. Tap **सहेजें**.

Adding a member needs a network connection. Savings and loans can still be recorded offline after members exist.

---

## 4. Record money (weekly meeting)

Open **जमा** at the bottom, or the green **जमा** / orange **कर्ज़** tiles on home.

### Choose the type

| Button | Hindi | When to use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green | **जमा** | Member pays weekly savings |
| Orange | **कर्ज़ दिया** | Group gives a loan to a member |
| Blue | **कर्ज़ चुकाया** | Member repays a loan |

### Enter the amount

1. Tap the type.
2. Type rupees on the big keypad (⌫ deletes).
3. Tap the member’s picture.
4. Tap **सहेजें और सुनें**.

The phone should speak Hindi, for example:

- *सीता ने 200 रुपये जमा किए*
- *गीता को 500 रुपये उधार दिए गए*
- *सीता ने 100 रुपये कर्ज़ चुकाया*

If there is no sound, check volume and that the browser allows speech.

---

## 5. Read the home numbers

The green box on **घर** (home) shows:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **हाथ में नकद** | Cash the group should have: savings + repayments − loans given |
| **कुल बचत** | Total savings recorded for all members |
| **बाकी कर्ज़** | Loans still outstanding |

Each member tile also shows their savings, and remaining loan if any.

These numbers come from the entries you typed. If cash in the box does not match, check **खाता** for a wrong entry.

## 6. Check the ledger

Tap **खाता** (history).

- Each row is one entry: name, amount, type, time.
- **सिंक बाकी** means the entry is on this phone and has not reached the server yet.
- Tap **सिंक** to send pending entries when the network is back.

---

## 7. Work without internet

You can record **जमा / कर्ज़ / चुकौती** while offline. Entries stay on the phone.

| Banner | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Dark bar: **ऑफलाइन — एंट्री फोन पर सेव होंगी** | No network. Keep recording. |
| Yellow bar: **X एंट्री सिंक बाकी** | Network is back. Tap the bar (or **सिंक**) to upload. |

A number badge on home or history is the count of unsynced entries.

**Adding a new member** still needs internet.

After sync, another phone that joined the same group can pull the same ledger.

## 8. Put the app on the home screen

If the green bar **ऐप होम स्क्रीन पर लगाएं** appears, tap **इंस्टॉल**.

On Android Chrome you can also use the browser menu → **Add to Home screen** / **Install app**. The app then opens like a normal app, without the browser address bar.

## 9. Sign out

On home, tap **बाहर**. You will need the group code and PIN to open the ledger again on this phone.

## Safety

- Share the PIN only with people who already handle the paper register.
- समूह is a record, not a wallet. Do not collect UPI or bank passwords in the app.
- Keep the paper notebook until the group trusts the digital ledger.
- If you suspect a wrong entry, check **खाता** with the group in the next meeting.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| **गलत पिन** | Recheck the 4 digits. PIN cannot be recovered from the screen. |
| **समूह नहीं मिला** | Recheck the 6-character code. Letters look similar (avoid 0/O). |
| No Hindi voice | Unmute, use Chrome, tap **सहेजें और सुनें** again. |
| Entries missing on a second phone | On the first phone tap **सिंक**, then open the second phone with network on. |
| Member will not save | Connect to internet, then add the member. |
| Yellow sync badge stays | Tap **सिंक** or the yellow bar. Confirm the phone is online. |

---

## What this app does not do

- Pay or receive money (no UPI, no bank transfer)
- Score credit or decide who gets a loan
- Connect to government NRLM systems
- Speak languages other than Hindi (v1)
