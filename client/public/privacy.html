Privacy policy

Last updated 22 September 2026

Safar is a travel planning app. This policy explains what we collect, what we send to other companies, and what stays on your phone. Google Play requires this page to be public.

Who we are

Safar (Android package com.safar.travel) is operated by the developer who published the Play Store listing. Contact: the support email on that listing.

What stays on your device

Safar also keeps a copy of your profile, chats, and trips on this phone so the app works offline. Logging out clears that copy. Your account copy stays in our database until you ask us to delete it.

Google account

You must sign in with Google to use Safar. Google shares your name, email, and profile photo. We store those plus your chats and saved trips so they reload on another device. Log out from Profile. We do not get your Google password.

What we send over the internet

  • Google Gemini — questions you ask and plan details (destination, dates, travelers, budget, notes, profile) go to our server, then to Google so the model can reply. Do not type passport numbers, card numbers, or passwords.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim / Overpass — the place you type is used to look up cities and listings. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
  • Open-Meteo — destination coordinates for weather.
  • Wikipedia — a short public city summary.
  • Safar activity log — our server stores the name and home city you typed, a device id, timestamps, and what you asked or planned (destination or a short question snippet). Plan searches (destination, dates, travelers, budget, style) are stored even if you do not tap Save. The operator account can review activity and chat transcripts to run the product. Kept up to 90 days. We do not sell it.

Booking.com, Airbnb, Zomato, and Google Maps open in the browser when you tap through. Safar does not take hotel payments.

AI-generated content

Itineraries, budgets, and visa steps are generated by AI and can be wrong. Visa steps are information only. You apply yourself on the official government site. Safar is not a visa agent, airline, or insurer.

Children

Safar is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

Edit your profile, delete trips, start a new chat, or tap Delete account in Profile. That removes your Google-linked profile, chats, and saved trips from Safar. You can also uninstall. We do not sell personal data. We do not show third-party ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date on this page.
