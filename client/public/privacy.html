Aurea · com.aurea.travel

Privacy policy

Last updated 19 September 2026

Aurea is a travel planning app. This policy explains what we collect, what we send to other companies, and what stays on your phone. Google Play requires this page to be public.

Who we are

Aurea (Android package com.aurea.travel) is operated by the developer who published the Play Store listing. Contact: the support email on that listing.

What stays on your device

Your name, home city, currency, chat history, and saved trips are stored on this phone. We do not create an account. Clearing app data or uninstalling deletes that history unless you shared a copy.

What we send over the internet

  • Google Gemini — questions you ask and plan details (destination, dates, travelers, budget, notes, profile) go to our server, then to Google so the model can reply. Do not type passport numbers, card numbers, or passwords.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim / Overpass — the place you type is used to look up cities and listings. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
  • Open-Meteo — destination coordinates for weather.
  • Wikipedia — a short public city summary.
  • Aurea activity log — our server stores the name and home city you typed, a device id, timestamps, and what you asked or planned (destination or a short question snippet) so we can operate the app. Kept up to 90 days. We do not sell it.

Booking.com, Airbnb, Zomato, and Google Maps open in the browser when you tap through. Aurea does not take hotel payments.

AI-generated content

Itineraries, budgets, and visa steps are generated by AI and can be wrong. Visa steps are information only. You apply yourself on the official government site. Aurea is not a visa agent, airline, or insurer.

Children

Aurea is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

Edit your profile, delete trips, start a new chat, or uninstall. We do not sell personal data. We do not show third-party ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date on this page.
